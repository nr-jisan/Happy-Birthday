PHOTO REEL — HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS

Put up to 6 portrait photos in this folder using these names:
photo1.jpg
photo2.jpg
photo3.jpg
photo4.jpg
photo5.jpg
photo6.jpg

JPG and PNG are recommended. If a photo is missing, the built-in sample placeholder will appear.

For the nicest reel effect, use vertical/portrait photos (around 2:3 or 9:16).
You can also edit the little captions in index.html inside the `photos` array.
